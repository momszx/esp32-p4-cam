## 1. Chip variants, package, modules

### Package and variants
- Package: **QFN104, 10 x 10 mm** [WS: preliminary datasheet version, CNX/Espressif].
- Variants: **ESP32-P4NRW16X** and **ESP32-P4NRW32X** (16 MB / 32 MB PSRAM in the package, stacked) [WS]. The earlier `ESP32-P4NRW16` / `ESP32-P4NRW32` are the old names for the v1.x chip revision; according to a forum post the NRW32 (v1.3) is EOL, the NRW32X (v3.x) is in mass production [WS, forum, unreliable]. On the P4NRWxx types the flash is **external** (no in-package flash) [UNVERIFIED – likely based on the "NR" designation: N=?, R=PSRAM; check the datasheet ordering table].
- A variant without PSRAM / with external PSRAM also exists (P4NRW16X "optional built-in PSRAM" remark in the PCN) [WS – vague].

### Chip revision v1.x vs v3.x
- Espressif PCN: v1.0, v1.1, v1.3 **not recommended for new designs**; v3.0/v3.1, and more recently **v3.2**, recommended [WS: PCN202600801 and forum].
- **Hardware difference: pin 54** is NC on v1.x, **a VDD_HP_1 power pin from v3.0 on** [WS: Espressif HW guidelines + v3.x user guide]. Extra components on the reference schematic: **1 MΩ resistor on the USB DP pin, 2 pcs 499 kΩ, 1 pc 22 pF capacitor in the DCDC circuit** [WS].
- The USB DP pull-down recommendation for v1.x applies only to v1.0/v1.3 [WS].
- v3.2: higher clock speed, better image processing, power management and security [WS, PCN]. **For a new design: v3.x (ESP32-P4NRW16X/NRW32X) is mandatory** – v1.3 stocks are being phased out.
- Consequence: if we prototype with any v1.x ready-made module (e.g. an old Waveshare one), the pin-54 handling of the final PCB may differ.

## 2. GPIO / pin summary

- GPIO count: **55 physical GPIOs (GPIO0–GPIO54)** according to the ESP-IDF v5.5+ documentation; the earlier v5.2 doc stated 57 (GPIO0–56) – v5.5+ is the current one [WS: ESP-IDF GPIO doc].
- **Strapping pins: GPIO34, 35, 36, 37, 38** [WS: ESP-IDF GPIO doc, confirmed]. On the EV board schematic GPIO35_BOOTMODE appears as the boot-mode signal [WS].
- Meaning of the strapping levels (**[UNVERIFIED], from memory**):
  - GPIO35 – boot mode (pull-up by default: SPI boot; held low when CHIP_PU is released: download mode)
  - GPIO36 – controls ROM log / bootloader messages
  - GPIO37 – UART0 TX / in some documentation "ROM print" (not certain)
  - GPIO38 – UART0 RX
  - GPIO34 – separate strap (JTAG/USB-JTAG source selection or PSRAM/flash voltage – **not certain**)
  The table must be taken from the datasheet's "Boot Configurations / Strapping Pins" chapter.
- UART0 download mode: GPIO37 (TX) / GPIO38 (RX) [UNVERIFIED – from memory, consistent with the strapping group].
- USB-Serial-JTAG: **GPIO24 (D−) / GPIO25 (D+)** [UNVERIFIED – from memory]. These pins go to the USB 1.1 Full-Speed PHY.
- USB 2.0 High-Speed OTG: **dedicated analog pins** (USB_DP / USB_DM, supplied from VDD_USBPHY), not GPIO-multiplexed [WS: a datasheet excerpt mentions VDD_USBPHY; the pin number is not confirmed].
- MIPI-CSI and MIPI-DSI: dedicated differential pin pairs (CSI: CLK± + 2 data pairs, DSI: CLK± + 2 data pairs) with the VDD_MIPI_DPHY supply [WS; pin numbers UNVERIFIED]. The P4 **CSI is 2-lane** [WS: ESP-IDF forum example data_lane_num = 2].
- SDIO host (SDMMC slot): the ESP32-P4 offers two SDMMC slots; on the Function-EV-Board slot 1 goes to the C6 (Zephyr doc: "C6 on SDIO slot 1, microSD on slot 0") [WS]. Slot 1 GPIOs: see 7.
- Flash: external SPI flash (HP domain) – pins [UNVERIFIED].
- LP IO (RTC) domain: VDDPST_1 3.3 V (preliminary datasheet v0.1), HP IO VDDPST_2 1.8/3.3 V [WS]. The exact division of the IO groups [UNVERIFIED] – the datasheet's "Power Supply / IO Pins" table is needed.
- HP IO supply voltage range: 1.65–1.95 V / 3.0–3.6 V, max 100 mA [WS: HW guidelines].

- Internal LDOs [WS]: HP LDO 1.1 V, LP LDO 1.1 V, Flash LDO 1.8/3.3 V, PSRAM LDO 1.9 V, VO3 LDO (0.5–2.7 V or 3.3 V, 50 mA).
- **An external 1.1 V (core) DCDC is required** for VDD_HP (not just an LDO!) – the Espressif guide advises against supplying the chip's main power from an LDO [WS]. This can also be done on a 2-layer board, but the DCDC loop must be minimised.
- Reference schematic for v3.x: **1 MΩ on USB DP, 2 x 499 kΩ + 22 pF in the DCDC circuit** [WS].
- Layout/decoupling recommendation [WS, third-party summary: lst-iot]: VDD_HP_0..3 trace ≥ 20 mil; 10 µF at the supply input, 100 nF next to every supply pin.
- Current budget (**[UNVERIFIED]**): with core + MIPI + PSRAM in active use, an expected 300–500 mA peak for the chip; together with the camera + C6, a 3.3 V branch of at least 1.5–2 A is recommended. Exact value: datasheet "Electrical Characteristics".
- Power-up sequence [WS]: CHIP_PU must be released high **after** the 3.3 V rails have risen, t_STBL min. 50 µs; reset low level min. t_RST = 1000 µs [WS: HW guidelines]. The sequencing of VDD_HP (1.1 V) depends on the DCDC [UNVERIFIED].

## 4. Clock

- 40 MHz main crystal [UNVERIFIED – usual in the ESP32 family; as far as I know the P4 is the same], typical 10 pF load; per the layout guideline the crystal is placed close to the chip, surrounded by ground.
- 32.768 kHz external crystal optional (RTC/LP), the internal RC is the default [UNVERIFIED].
- The camera sensor (OV5640) requires its own XCLK (6–27 MHz): the P4 has a LEDC/CLK_OUT output, or a separate crystal/oscillator on the sensor side [UNVERIFIED; design decision].

## 5. Programming / debug

- CHIP_PU (EN): RC delay **R = 10 kΩ, C = 1 µF** (1 µF since HW guidelines v1.9, 2026-07-21; previously 0.1 µF) [WS]. Must not float. Reset threshold: low level below 0.25 x VDD_BAT. Short trace [WS]. For slow-rising/battery supplies an external supervisor (~3.0 V) is recommended [WS].
- Flashing: USB-Serial-JTAG (GPIO24/25, [UNVERIFIED]) or UART0 (GPIO37/38, [UNVERIFIED]). EV board v1.5.2 already uses USB-Serial-JTAG, without CP2102N [WS].
- Download mode: pull GPIO35 low when CHIP_PU is released [UNVERIFIED – supported by the EV board schematic's "GPIO35_BOOTMODE" name [WS]].
- An auto-reset (DTR/RTS) circuit is only needed with a UART bridge; not with USB-Serial-JTAG. Buttons: EN and BOOT (GPIO35) are sufficient.

## 6. Camera interfaces

- **MIPI-CSI**: 2 data lanes [WS: the ESP-IDF forum used as the source]. Lane bit rate: an official CSI value is **not confirmed**; for DSI, 2 lanes x 1.5 Gbps is documented [WS: Espressif MIPI-DSI summary]. According to the forum, CSI is ~1 Gbps/lane in practice, with partial frames at 972 Mbps [WS, forum]. For the OV5640 on 2 lanes (configuration up to max ~1 Gbps) this is sufficient for 1080p30; the P4 ISP processes the raw/YUV images [UNVERIFIED details].
- **DVP (parallel)**: available. The ESP-IDF camera driver documentation mentions "ISP DVP" (8 bit, PCLK, HSYNC, VSYNC, DE) and, in newer versions, an LCD_CAM DVP port; example: `dvp_isp_dsi` [WS]. The DVP GPIO assignment (GPIO matrix vs fixed) **[UNVERIFIED]** – the ISP DVP probably goes through the GPIO matrix, but this must be checked.
- Conclusion: the OV5640 is best used over MIPI-CSI (2 lanes) (fewer wires, 100 Ω diff pairs); DVP as a fallback for a 2-layer, simpler routing.

## 7. ESP32-C6 link (esp-hosted)

- esp-hosted-mcu, SDIO (4-bit) link based on the Function-EV-Board (according to a third-party component readme, "identical to the Espressif EV board wiring") [WS]:
  | Signal | P4 GPIO | C6 side |
  |---|---|---|
  | SDIO D0..D3 | GPIO14..GPIO17 | IO20..IO23 |
  | SDIO CLK | GPIO18 | IO19 |
  | SDIO CMD | GPIO19 | IO18 |
  | C6 CHIP_PU/EN | GPIO54 | EN |
  | C6 flash/UART (IO9 boot, UART header) | – | IO9 (boot), UART TX/RX |
- The EV board schematic shows the nets `SD2_CLK/CMD/D0..D3` and `C6_EN`, `C6_WAKEUP` [WS]. The exact P4 pin connection of the nets could not be read from the PDF -> **must be checked** on the v1.5.2 schematic.
- SDIO host on the P4: **SDMMC slot 1** (slot 0 is the microSD) [WS: Zephyr doc].
- Alternative SPI/UART in esp-hosted mode: [UNVERIFIED]; SDIO is the fastest and the default.
- For an inter-board (stack) connection the SDIO CLK is ~40–50 MHz, so a short, grounded path is needed, and a pin header is not ideal for fast SDIO [UNVERIFIED].
- To program the C6: a UART header (TX/RX/IO9/EN) is needed, or it can also be updated from the P4 via esp-hosted OTA [UNVERIFIED].

## 8. Layout constraints (2-layer PCB)

Sources: HW guidelines excerpts [WS], but I did not read the layout page directly – most of the following is [UNVERIFIED], from general RF/high-speed practice.

- MIPI CSI/DSI: 100 Ω differential impedance (general MIPI D-PHY requirement). Intra-pair length mismatch ≤ a few mil. Furthermore, on the P4 the lane rate is 1–1.5 Gbps -> a reference plane is needed.
- USB HS: 90 Ω differential. HS USB practically requires a continuous ground plane.
- **2-layer, 1.6 mm FR4:** for a 100 Ω diff pair (e.g. 0.2 mm trace / 0.2 mm gap) at 1.6 mm thickness the reference plane is 1.6 mm away, which is **not achievable** with a realistic trace width (the diff impedance goes above 150–200 Ω, or a 5+ mm wide pair would be needed). Not recommended.
- **2-layer, 0.8 mm:** better (the ground is 0.8 mm away), but still only approximate; 0.4–0.6 mm thick FR4 or an FPC camera is better. A coplanar diff pair (CPWG) with ground pour on the top layer helps. Realistic feasibility: short (<30 mm) MIPI; USB HS risky.
- The bare P4 QFN104 in a BGA-like MIPI/PSRAM/DCDC environment practically cannot be fanned out properly on 2 layers (104 pins, 10 x 10 mm, 0.4 mm pitch?). The pin pitch [UNVERIFIED].
- Exposed pad: GND, thermal/electrical; several vias recommended (e.g. 3 x 3 = 9 vias) [UNVERIFIED; check the exact number on the Espressif guideline page].
- Decoupling: 100 nF next to every VDD pin, VDD_HP min. 20 mil trace [WS].
- **Practical recommendation:** stacked design: lower/upper boards 2-layer, but the P4 and MIPI/USB HS section **must go on a 4-layer** board (or a ready-made module); connect the OV5640 via an FPC/camera module connector (no long MIPI on 2 layers).

## Open questions / risks
1. **Direct reading of the main sources did not happen** (WebFetch blocked). First task: manual download of the datasheet PDF + EV board schematic + HW guidelines; the [UNVERIFIED] items of chapters 2–8 must be checked, in particular: pin numbers, strapping table, VDDPST groups, VDD_MIPI/USB voltages.
2. Does the Waveshare module have a castellated/LGA version, and what are its exact size and pin list? Is it available with a v3.x chip? (For a 2-layer board, the module route is probably the only realistic one.)
3. v1.x vs v3.x: because of pin 54 and the extra DCDC/USB passives, the revision of the prototype module determines the design of our own PCB.
4. Chip availability: the v1.3 NRW32 is being phased out; sourcing the v3.x NRW16X/NRW32X (LCSC/Digikey/Mouser) is uncertain.
5. On a 2-layer 1.6 mm board the MIPI-CSI 100 Ω and USB HS 90 Ω cannot be held; a 4-layer PCB or a ready-made module + FPC camera is needed.
6. The official CSI max. lane bit rate is not confirmed (forum: ~1 Gbps); check the OV5640 2-lane configuration.
7. DVP GPIO assignment and the exact pin numbers of the CSI/DSI pins.
8. Stacked boards: SDIO (D0..D3, CLK, CMD, ~40 MHz) through a pin header is a signal-integrity risk; preferably the C6 + P4 on one board, with the camera/IO on a separate board.
9. Power: the exact current budget and the DCDC selection (the type recommended by Espressif) have not been extracted.
